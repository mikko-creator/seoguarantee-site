The fair question

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?

If you searched this category you have probably already read the article that answers yes. It is a serious article and most of it is correct, so here is the reply in the order it makes its case.

01

Conceded, and it goes first.

Google Search Central states: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." That is true, and it is quoted here as the objection rather than argued with. Nothing anybody does hands them control of a ranking algorithm, and treating that sentence as a technicality to route around would be the tell.

02

What is under contract is not the algorithm. It is the invoice.

Charging is the one thing this firm controls absolutely and on its own, so that is what the paperwork conditions. It is a commercial exposure taken on deliberately, on terms published before anybody signs, and it makes no assertion about how search works underneath.

03

So do not take it on faith.

We do not ask you to believe the promise. We ask you to check whether we delivered before you pay for it. The line by line reply to every part of that article, with the scope boundary set out beside it, is on the guarantee.
