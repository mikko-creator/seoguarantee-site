03

How each month is assessed.

  1. A monthly retainer is agreed up front.
  2. Each month is assessed against the metrics named in the contract.
  3. In a month we are off target, the work continues and your billing stops.
  4. Billing resumes when results are back on track.

It is a billing term, not a prediction, and nobody here is claiming to control how a search engine ranks anything. We keep working, you stop paying, and the obligation is suspended rather than erased.

One comparison is worth making, and only structurally. Every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer, and the ordinary version of that retainer is owed whether or not the agreed positions arrive. Ours is the one that stops being owed.
