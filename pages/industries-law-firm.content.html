The monthly assessment is what decides whether the invoice stands.

Crisis-driven matter search

An accident, an arrest, an injury. The search happens within hours, on a phone, and the person contacts two or three firms off the first page and then stops looking. Time to contact is short and name recognition is close to irrelevant.

Considered engagement search

Business litigation, contracts, estate planning, employment. Researched across days or weeks, usually by somebody comparing firms deliberately, and carrying qualifiers that a person in crisis never types.

The pause mechanic

Stated as a term, because a term is what it is.

The retainer is agreed at signing. Each month is assessed against the agreed metrics. A month that lands off-target pauses the billing, the scope of work continues without reduction, and billing resumes once results are back on track.

This is an obligation suspended by a clause that was in the engagement before anyone signed, and it operates on its own the moment a month falls short. The published attack on this category is that a remedy agency can keep your initial investment anyway. The answer is that the billing stops under a term you agreed in advance, not that something gets handed back after an argument.

Five questions

Answered in the register of an engagement letter.

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?

As a general proposition, yes, and a supplier promising you a position by a date deserves suspicion. We are not making a representation about how a search engine will behave. We are stating when a fee becomes payable.

Who names the phrases?

We propose them from a read of the current page one and they are named in the engagement you sign. You do not hold a veto over the list, and it is not open to substitution once signed.

What does a paused month actually mean for the work?

The scope continues in full. Nobody comes off the account, the schedule does not change, and the billing stops until the positions are back on track. It happens automatically under the clause, so there is nothing for you to invoke and nothing to negotiate.

How long?

The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, which are not yet an agreed contract term. The engagement is checked against the named phrases every month throughout, never as a single test at the end.

What happens on the call?

A review of your market, the practice areas and jurisdictions in scope, and a proposed phrase list. It is not a branded product, it does not have a name, and nothing is signed on it.
