Five questions

The whole objection load, in one place.

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?

The published warnings describe a real pattern and you should take them seriously. Nobody can promise a specific position on the page, and we do not. What is guaranteed here is a term of business about when an invoice is owed. No search engine approves of, permits or endorses this arrangement, and nothing on this site pretends one does.

What will you do, and what will you not do?

Done: technical repair of the site, a page for each contracted phrase, the local profile and its categories, service-area definition, internal linking, review flow, and the conversion work on the call path. Not done: purchased links, private blog networks, spun or padded text, phrase stacking, hidden text, and fabricated reviews.

Who chooses the phrases?

We propose them from a read of who currently answers those searches, and they are named in the contract you sign. There is no veto and nothing is substituted after the contract is signed.

What does a paused month mean in the middle of peak season?

Nobody comes off the account, the schedule does not change, and the season is not treated as a reason to accept less. What stops is the billing, until results are back on track. The clause is in the contract before signature, so it operates on its own rather than being something you have to raise while you are busy.

How long?

The 6- and 12-month windows we state are not yet an agreed contract term, and the agreement is assessed month by month throughout. The industry's published realism band is three to six months and the pattern buyers are warned about is page one in 30 days, so the longer stated window is the honest one. A campaign starting in spring and a campaign starting in autumn will look different at month three, and the monthly assessment is what keeps that from becoming an argument between us.
