Why the trade decides it

A guarantee written against the wrong phrases would be worthless to us.

The published case against guaranteed search work leans hardest on a single charge, that one-size-fits-all strategies do not work, and the charge is fair. It is also the charge we can least afford, because we are only paid while the agreed metrics are being delivered. An agency on an unconditional retainer can survive a generic phrase list indefinitely. We cannot.

Four things change completely from one trade to the next.

The phrase set.

A homeowner types the fault, a patient types the symptom, somebody in trouble types the event, and none of them types the name of the category they are about to buy from.

The buying moment.

An emergency at eleven at night and a purchase researched across three weeks are not the same demand wearing different clothes. One is settled before the second result finishes loading. The other is settled in a comparison you are not present for.

The geography that decides it.

A radius around one chair, a drive time a truck can actually cover, a metro, or a practice licensed across two states. The boundary is part of what gets contracted.

What a lead is worth.

This is what decides which phrases we propose in the first place. A phrase worth a service call and a phrase worth a matter that runs for a year do not deserve the same effort.
