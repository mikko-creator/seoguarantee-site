The reasoning

This is the part that makes the bundling logical rather than generous.

The page that ranks is a page on your site

We are contracted to reach page one for named key phrases, and the thing that arrives there is a template, a layout and a piece of writing that you own.

The rebuild risk

Builds that do not cost you rankings.

A redesign is the most common way a business loses positions it already had.

Here is what we hold to on a rebuild.

01

URLs and redirects are planned before launch rather than reconstructed afterwards from what broke.

02

Content and internal links are carried over deliberately, page by page, rather than left behind in the old template.

03

Page speed is treated as a design constraint while the design is being made, not as a cleanup task after it ships.

04

And nothing goes live that the site cannot then be measured on.

There is a fair test of all of this: put an agency's own website through a speed test before you believe anything it says about yours. This page has to pass the standards it just claimed.
