Demand sits in two layers.

Category phrases

Med spa with a city, medical spa with a city, medical spa near me. Fewer searches than most owners assume.

Treatment phrases

Body contouring, laser treatments, injectables, skin resurfacing, hair removal, each with a city or a near me attached. Collectively far larger, fragmented across many separate phrases, and where the decision is actually made. They are also where your service menu genuinely differs from the spa down the road.

Geography: aesthetic clients will travel further than a dental patient and still not across a metro, and demand concentrates around particular neighbourhoods and around the dates people book ahead of. The catchment is something the consultation defines against your own book.

Five questions

The whole objection load, answered here.

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?

The published warnings are right about the pattern they describe, and search engines say plainly that no one can guarantee a given ranking. This is a term of business about when an invoice is owed, not a claim about controlling a search engine. No search engine approves of, permits or endorses this arrangement, and nothing here suggests otherwise.

What tactics do you use, and what will you not do?

Performed: technical repair of the site, a page for each contracted treatment phrase, the local profile and its categories, internal linking, review flow, and the work on the booking path. Refused: purchased links, private blog networks, spun or padded text, phrase stacking, hidden text, and fabricated reviews. We do not review or clear your advertising for regulatory compliance, and nothing we do should be treated as that review.

Who chooses which procedures we go after?

We propose them from a read of who currently holds page one, and they are named in the contract you sign. You do not hold a veto over the list and cannot swap a phrase in after signature.

What does a paused month mean?

The work continues at full scope, the billing stops, and it resumes once results are back on track. The clause is in the contract before signature, so it operates by itself and there is nothing for you to claim.

How long?

The 6- and 12-month windows we state are not yet an agreed contract term, and the agreement is assessed month by month throughout. The industry's own published realism band is three to six months and the pattern buyers are warned about is page one in 30 days, so the longer stated window is the honesty signal.
