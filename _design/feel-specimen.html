Colophon, what this specimen claims and what it refuses

How the light surface was separated, and what was left out

  1. The value ladder was refused, not adapted. The owner-chosen structural reference separates three near-black grounds about seven units apart. Measured live in the browser, this surface's light grounds sit at 1.107:1 paper to tint and 1.011:1 tint to warm. The second pair is optically the same luminance. A value ladder does none of the work here, so it is demoted to a supporting hue cue and never asked to carry a boundary.
  2. SEP-1 hairline plus SEP-2 contact shadow do the work instead. Every light-to-light seam carries a 1px purple-tinted rule, measured at 1.368:1 against paper and 1.236:1 against the tint, plus a 20px graded ink ramp directly beneath it. The line states the boundary and the ramp gives it a direction, so the band above reads as resting on the band below rather than merely ending. Into the anchor and into the gold, where the measured ground step is already 17.61:1 and 11.27:1, both devices are switched off rather than left on out of habit.
  3. SEP-3 the framed media well. Every rights-clear founder file is a transparent cutout, which on white has no edge at all at the boundary. The well supplies one: a tinted plate, a token grid material, a hairline and a two-stop ink-tinted shadow. Neither photo is scaled past its native pixels.
  4. SEP-4 the structural accent rule, SEP-5 the three-edge card. Each band opens on a 2px deep-gold rule at 8.40:1, an unambiguous dark-on-light edge. Each card carries a hairline, a one-pixel inset top highlight and a two-stop ink-tinted shadow: three edge events per card boundary rather than none.
  5. SEP-6 material, SEP-7 range. Planes at the same value but different material still separate, so the hero carries a token grid and the anchor carries token grain, both at the faint opacity step. The near-black anchor and the gold band then supply the luminance range, which is what teaches the eye what a plane is before it reads the near-identical light ones.
  6. Tokens were spent, never minted. Zero new custom properties, zero raw brand hex. The purple glow wash is the locked token dialled to the faint opacity step, because the same value that reads as atmosphere on near-black reads as a stain on paper. That inversion is the general rule this specimen is arguing for.
  7. The one 1920px asset was refused. The only hero-resolution file on disk is a dated infographic with words baked into the pixels. It cannot be restyled, it reprints copy the build must own, and it belongs to the execution the direction says to leave behind. No stock wash was substituted for it.
  8. What does not appear, and may not. No price of any kind. No metro count. No success rate, percentage or aggregate total. No client name, client logo or press logo. No refund, money-back or risk-free language, because billing pauses and resumes. No claim that the pause has ever been operated on a live account. No exclamation mark and no emoji.
  9. Still open. The hero animation slot is a fixed-ratio well here on purpose: the ratified poster and loop are governed by their own engineering rule and are not composed into this specimen. The logo vector and founder photography at retina resolution are still owed, so every lockup remains typeset and every portrait remains small and framed.
