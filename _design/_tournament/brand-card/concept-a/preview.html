Clause two

Palette, by role

Seventeen colour roles. Every ratio printed here was computed this pass against these exact hexes with the WCAG relative-luminance formula, and against the ground the role is actually used on. Where a role fails, the register says so rather than omitting it.

The one documented exception to the fixed measure: this register runs the full sheet width. A register is not prose. The exception exists so that the measure reads as a decision everywhere else.

The attested ground

Dark purple chrome, modernized into a ground

The live site's structural dark purple measures #4b0670. It clears contrast at 13.47:1, but it is a mid purple rather than a ground, and that is exactly why the old chrome reads dated. Pushed down to a purple-tinted near-black it becomes an anchor: the same brand, quieter and deeper. This block is hard-edged by rule, with no radius softening it, and it carries a gold hairline at its top edge.

Clause five

The clause rule

The signature motif, isolated, with its construction legible. A single hairline baseline carries one tick per monthly assessment; the assessed month is a taller purple tick with a square node; the rule terminates in a solid gold segment and a square seal. It is drawn, never iconified, and it illustrates the mechanic without reporting anybody's history.

  • --ds-lineThe twelve monthly ticks. Equal weight, equal spacing: no month is more important than another.
  • --ds-primaryThe assessed month, raised, with a square node. Purple is the structural accent, so it marks structure.
  • --ds-accentThe terminus and its square seal. Gold appears once, at the end, and is the smallest area of colour on the sheet.

The same motif, built in CSS at working scale

The rule scales to any width, needs no asset, and costs nothing to paint, which matters more here than on most brands, because prospects run a performance audit on this vendor's own site as basic due diligence.
