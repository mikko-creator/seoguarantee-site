Palette, 17 roles

Purple structures. Gold punctuates.

Purple #7f2199 and gold #ffba00 are measured from the live site, not chosen. Every derived value below was computed this pass against the exact hexes in the token sheet. Each band prints its ratio against the ground it is actually used on.

One binding rule sits above the arithmetic: neither accent is ever body text, on either canvas. The purple clears 8.14:1 on white and is still barred, because that headroom buys eyebrows, labels, links and icons, never paragraphs.

That is why gold in this identity is a surface you stand on rather than a colour you write in, and why white never appears on a gold ground anywhere.

The month strip

Twelve cells, one per assessed month. Filled means on target. The hollow cell is the month the work continues and the billing stops. This diagram illustrates the mechanic. It does not report any client’s history, and no cell here corresponds to a real account.

Why a strip and not a chartA chart would imply a measured history. A strip states a rule. The mechanic is the claim, and it is the only claim here.

Construction
Twelve equal cells on a 3:4 ratio, six-pixel gutter, six-pixel control radius. Nothing is weighted, because no month outranks another.
The hollow cell
Two-pixel gold inset ring, transparent fill, one gold bar across the midline. The absence is drawn, not implied by a paler fill.
Where it recurs
Anywhere the cadence is explained. It scales to three cells inline, and to a single cell as a status marker.

Colophon, what this sheet does not claim

Open items, stated rather than hidden

  1. The logo vector is still owed. LOGO-VECTOR: NEEDS-OWNER. Every lockup on this sheet is typeset in Montserrat because no vector exists in any supplied or scraped source and the largest raster is 403 px wide.
  2. The palette is proposed, not ratified. The white-dominant ground with purple and gold accents remains provisional and awaits confirmation against a rendered specimen. This sheet is that specimen.
  3. No price appears anywhere, including a “from” figure. There is no pricing atom in this system and no tier card was drawn, even as a card specimen.
  4. No client name, client logo, press logo or client mockup appears. The strongest assets on disk are rights-blocked and stay blocked until written permission is on file.
  5. No invented percentage, success rate, aggregate total or metro count appears, and none may. The shape is refused, not only the number.
  6. Photography is at the resolution that exists. Every founder image on file is web resolution, so the vignette uses a 92 px portrait chip and never a full-bleed. Real photography at retina resolution is still owed.
  7. Terminology is fixed. First page, never a position claim. Key phrases, and only ever key phrases. The guarantee, singular and definite, because there is exactly one. Full Suite, capitalised. Complementary, spelled with an e.
  8. Contact, rendered identically everywhere it appears: 6905 W Charleston Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89117, USA · +1 (702) 420-7272 · support@seoguarantee.com · seoguarantee.com
