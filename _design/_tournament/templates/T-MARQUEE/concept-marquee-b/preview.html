01 The terms, stated

We guarantee first page for the key phrases named in your contract, assessed month by month, and any month we are off target we keep working and your billing stops.

The guarantee, in the language of the contract: what is agreed, how it is measured, and when you stop paying.

A monthly retainer, agreed up front. Every month is assessed against the metrics named in your contract. In a month we deliver them, the month is invoiced. In a month we do not, the invoicing stops and the campaign carries on at our cost.

06 What first page means

What "first page" means here.

First page means page one of Google organic results for an agreed key phrase in an agreed geography.

That is the whole definition, and the offer turns on it. It is not a claim about a specific position on that page. It is not a claim about paid placement, map results or AI-generated answers, and none of those enters the metric unless your contract names them.

The instrument

The reporting instrument already exists.

A monthly keyword page-ranking report lists every phrase on your contract's phrase schedule with its position for that month. It is the same document the assessment in S2 is read from, which is the point: the thing that decides the invoice is a document you also hold.

07 A month off target

A month we are off target.

Three things happen, and the third is the one the category does not offer.

  1. It is automatic, and it was written in before you signed. The pause is a contract trigger, not a concession you have to ask for after a bad quarter. Nobody at this firm decides whether you have complained enough to deserve it. That is the entire distinction between this and the remedies buyers are warned about in this category, which are discretionary by design and can be declined.
  2. Nothing is handed back, because nothing was charged. The obligation for that month is suspended, not erased after the fact. There is no reversal to chase and no credit note to negotiate, because the invoice for that month does not go out.
  3. The work does not stop. The billing does. A month that is not invoiced is worked exactly like a month that is. Invoicing resumes on the condition the REMEDY ON MISS row in S2 states.

The honest comparison.

The strongest agencies in this market offer month-to-month contracts and a commitment to consistent communication and complete transparency. That is real, and it is worth something: you are free to leave at any point. But freedom to leave is not a condition on the fee. The client still carries the whole outcome risk while the retainer is owed either way. That is a structural fact about how the category is sold, not a criticism of anyone selling it.

The mechanic above stands on the agreement, not on a story. It needs no precedent to be true, and we do not offer one. See S12 for why.

08 What it replaces

What a conditional retainer replaces, and what it collapses.

Replaces

The unconditional monthly retainer. Hourly SEO consulting. Per-project SEO invoices. And the remedies this category offers instead of a payment condition: money returned at the agency's discretion, a period of free work, or a different piece of work in place of the one that missed. Each of those leaves the agency holding what you already invested, which is precisely why the category's own critics dismiss them.

Collapses

The separately retained stack. One agreed monthly fee covers SEO, website design and development, content writing, reputation management, conversion optimisation, graphic design, branding and consulting.

The causal order matters and it runs one way only. We are paid on the metric rather than on the hours, so every part of the stack is pointed at the one thing you are paying for. Holding the web work or the conversion work back to sell it separately would only make our own job harder and our own invoice less likely. The breadth is not generosity and it is not a bonus. It is what outcome-pricing forces.

You will find no figure on this page, on either side of that comparison, including anyone else's. The comparison runs on structure alone. One agreed monthly fee covers SEO, web, CRO, branding and design, quoted per business after a competitive review.

10 The seven answers

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag? Seven answers, in the order the case against it is usually made.

If you have read the standard warning article about this category, you have read seven arguments. Here they are in order, answered on their merits.

01

Search engines warn against guaranteed rankings. Conceded, and it goes first.

Google Search Central states: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." Google's own beginner documentation goes further: "If they guarantee you that their changes will give you first place in search results, find someone else." Both are correct, and neither is a technicality we are about to work around. Nothing we do gives us control of a ranking algorithm, Google does not approve, endorse or permit a promise of position, and we make no suggestion otherwise. What is under our control is our own invoice. That is what the contract conditions.

02

You cannot make a guarantee about something you cannot control. Correct, and we agree.

Which is why what we guarantee is not the algorithm. It is the billing. We control when we bill, absolutely and unilaterally, and we have written into the contract that we do not bill in a month that fails the assessment in S2.

03

A guaranteed service may involve tactics that risk a penalty.

This is the fair version of the objection, because a fee tied to a position is a fee that rewards shortcuts. The answer is the published method in S5 and the standing commitment in S11: we do not use a tactic that puts your domain at risk of a search penalty. Note also what the billing basis does to the incentive. A penalty ends the positions, which ends the invoice, and the recovery work would then run at our cost for as long as it took.

04

Guaranteed rankings may provide little benefit.

The specific charge is that the guaranteed phrases are the easy ones. S5 is the answer: the phrases are proposed from competitive research, each for a reason we state out loud, and put on the schedule you sign, so you can read exactly what your money is tied to and judge whether those phrases would produce work for you.

05

One-size-fits-all strategies do not work.

Agreed, and the trade-specific pages are where that is answered rather than asserted. See the pages for dental practices, law firms, medical spas, HVAC businesses and contracting businesses. Each carries the substance of that trade, because a dental practice and an HVAC business are not competing for the same kind of search at the same moment.

06

The wrong tactics can hurt your business.

Also true, which is why S11 exists and why it states what this guarantee does not cover as plainly as the terms table states what it does.

07

Page-one rankings in 30 days.

This is the checkable one. The 6- and 12-month windows quoted and attributed in S6 sit inside the three-to-six-month band the category's own critics publish as realistic, and the agreement itself is assessed on the monthly cadence in S2.

And one more

One thing we will not claim.

This offer is a guaranteed SEO service by the standard definition: a service promising a specific result, typically first-page rankings for a set of key phrases, inside a timeframe. We are inside that category, not beside it, and saying otherwise would be falsifiable in a single search.

The reframe this page was built to earn

We are not asking you to believe the promise. We are asking you to check whether we delivered, before you pay for it.

11 The boundary

What this guarantee does not cover.

A terms page with no boundary is a terms page hiding something. This section is the boundary.

Part one

What we will not do.

We do not use a tactic that puts your domain at risk of a search penalty. That is a standing commitment about our own conduct, not a list of anyone else's sins.

Part two

What the guarantee is not.

These are already ruled and ship as written.

  • It is not a promise of a specific position on page one. First page means what S6 defines it to mean, and nothing narrower.
  • It is not a promise of a sales figure, a revenue number or a lead volume. We make no claim about what your business will earn.
  • It is not money returned to you. Billing pauses and later resumes, and the obligation is suspended rather than erased.
  • It is not a promise about paid placement, map results or AI-generated answers, unless your contract names them.

Nothing from the unsubstantiated claim set on the business's existing website is imported into this page to prop up the guarantee, in this section or anywhere else on it.

14 The questions still owed

The questions this page still owes you.

How long is the agreement?

The term length is a contract question that has not been ruled, and it is not guessed here. What is settled is the cadence in S2: each month is billed or paused on its own result.

What counts as back on track?

The month clears the same assessment the pause was triggered by, read from the same monthly ranking report. It is the same test in both directions, which is what stops it being a judgement call.

Who verifies the ranking, and in what tool?

Not ruled. This is a contract term and it is flagged rather than answered. Ask on the call, and hold us to the answer in writing.

What happens if I change my site mid-campaign?

Tell us first. Site changes are part of the work and most of them are ours to make anyway, since web and development sit inside the same fee. A change made without us that moves a contracted position is the kind of thing the agreement has to account for, which is another reason the scope boundary in S11 needs the owner's ruling rather than ours.

What if my competitors outspend me?

Spend is not the metric and it is not what the contract is judged on. If a phrase becomes undeliverable for your business, the honest moment to say so is at the consultation, before it is on the schedule. If it becomes undeliverable later, the billing consequence is the one in S2: we are off target, so we keep working and you stop paying.

What happens to the work I have already had if I leave?

What transfers on exit, and on what terms, has not been ruled. It belongs in the contract and it is not invented here.

Is the consultation free?

The entry offer has never been defined in the business's own copy, so it is not branded or priced here. What is described plainly is the thing itself: a conversation about the phrases your competitors currently hold in your market, and whether they are ones we can commit to.
