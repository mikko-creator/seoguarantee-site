On the record

What we are contracted to deliver.

This is the part most agencies leave vague, so here it is in order.

  1. The agency proposes and the client signs. They come out of competitive research into what your market actually searches and what your competitors already hold, and they are named in the contract you sign.
  2. "First page" has one meaning here. It is page one of Google organic results for an agreed key phrase in an agreed geography. Not a position on a chart, not a phrase we picked afterwards because it moved.
  3. The assessment is monthly. The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, and the agreement itself is assessed month by month rather than as a single test at the end.

The situation

Someone else already owns the phrase your customers type.

The situation is usually specific rather than general. It is not that you need more traffic. It is that a competitor consistently holds the phrases your customers actually type, that position is producing their calls rather than yours, and the question you are really asking is what it takes to take it.

Now the harder half

If you searched for SEO services, you have probably already read a well-argued article telling you that a guaranteed ranking is a warning sign. Those articles exist for good reasons: the promise is usually vague, no metric is ever named, and there is no way for you to check afterwards whether it was met. We are not going to claim to sit outside that category. We are going to answer it by publishing the terms, which is what this page does and what the full argument on guarantee does at length.

Scope

What the SEO work covers.

Get traffic, generate high quality leads, convert those leads to sales. The search work below serves the first, and the rest of the Full Suite serves the other two.

Technical and on-page optimisation of the site itself

Structure, templates, internal linking, headings and the content on the pages that have to carry an agreed phrase. If a page cannot be crawled, read and understood, no amount of phrase research rescues it.

The Google Business Profile alongside the website

Both are targets, not one. For a business whose customers search locally, the profile is often the first result they interact with and the last one they judge you on, so it is worked rather than left to sit.

Content writing

The pages that hold an agreed key phrase need real writing, produced as part of the campaign rather than commissioned separately, and revised as the campaign learns which pages are doing the work.

Reputation management

What a searcher finds about you next, once the position has done its job and they start checking.

THE CADENCE

Ongoing monthly work rather than a one-time project.

There is no launch after which the campaign is finished. Each month has work in it, each month is assessed, and the plan moves with what the results say.

All of it is done in house. That is the practical difference most clients notice first: no needing multiple people, companies, freelancers or agencies, no outsourcing, and nobody in the chain who can be blamed by somebody else in the chain.

The cadence

How each month is assessed.

Four beats, and they are terms of business rather than claims about controlling an algorithm.

01

A monthly retainer is agreed up front.

02

Each month is assessed against the metrics named in the contract.

03

In a month we are off target, the work continues and your billing stops.

04

Billing resumes when results are back on track.

Say plainly what that is and what it is not. It is not a prediction, and nobody here is claiming to control how a search engine ranks anything. We keep working, you stop paying, and the obligation is suspended rather than erased.

One comparison is worth making, and only structurally. Every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer, and the ordinary version of that retainer is owed whether or not the agreed positions arrive. Ours is the one that stops being owed. We print no rates, ranges or hourly figures on either side of that comparison, because this site publishes no figures at all and quoting someone else's would only invite you to price ours against it.

The objection

No agency controls how a search engine ranks anything.

Start with the part of the objection that is true. No agency controls how a search engine ranks anything, and any page implying a search engine has blessed the practice of guaranteeing rankings is not telling you the truth. If you have read that argument, you read it correctly.

Here is the distinction the whole business rests on. This is not a claim of algorithmic certainty. It is a term about when an invoice is owed.

01

"The promise is vague."

Ours names the phrases in the contract before you sign it, and defines page one of Google organic results in an agreed geography as what is being measured.

02

"The remedy is discretionary."

Ours is automatic and monthly. It is triggered by the contract rather than conceded after a complaint, which is the difference between a term and a goodwill gesture.

03

"The agency keeps your money either way."

You are not paying for the attempt in those months.
