04 · The situation

Someone else already holds the phrase.

The situation is usually specific rather than general. It is not that you need more traffic.

It is that a competitor consistently holds the phrases your customers actually type, that position is producing their calls rather than yours, and the question you are really asking is what it takes to take it.

The category headwind

If you searched for SEO services, you have probably already read a well-argued article telling you that a guaranteed ranking is a warning sign.

Those articles exist for good reasons: the promise is usually vague, no metric is ever named, and there is no way for you to check afterwards whether it was met. We are not going to claim to sit outside that category. We are going to answer it by publishing the terms, which is what this page does and what the full argument on guarantee does at length.

05 · Scope of work

Scope of the search work.

The build objectives are the owner's own, in his order: get traffic, generate high quality leads, convert those leads to sales. The search work below serves the first, and the rest of the Full Suite serves the other two.

  1. Technical and on-page optimisation of the site itself. Structure, templates, internal linking, headings and the content on the pages that have to carry an agreed phrase. If a page cannot be crawled, read and understood, no amount of phrase research rescues it.
  2. The Google Business Profile alongside the website. Both are targets, not one. For a business whose customers search locally, the profile is often the first result they interact with and the last one they judge you on, so it is worked rather than left to sit.
  3. Content writing. The pages that hold an agreed key phrase need real writing, produced as part of the campaign rather than commissioned separately, and revised as the campaign learns which pages are doing the work.
  4. Reputation management. What a searcher finds about you next, once the position has done its job and they start checking.
  5. Ongoing monthly work rather than a one-time project. There is no launch after which the campaign is finished. Each month has work in it, each month is assessed, and the plan moves with what the results say.

All of it is done in house. That is the practical difference most clients notice first: no needing multiple people, companies, freelancers or agencies, no outsourcing, and nobody in the chain who can be blamed by somebody else in the chain.

06 · The assessment

How each month is assessed.

Four beats, and they are terms of business rather than claims about controlling an algorithm.

01

A monthly retainer is agreed up front.

02

Each month is assessed against the metrics named in the contract.

03

In a month we are off target, the work continues and your billing stops.

04

Billing resumes when results are back on track.

Say plainly what that is and what it is not. It is not a prediction, and nobody here is claiming to control how a search engine ranks anything.

One comparison is worth making, and only structurally. Every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer, and the ordinary version of that retainer is owed whether or not the agreed positions arrive. Ours is the one that stops being owed. We print no rates, ranges or hourly figures on either side of that comparison, because this site publishes no figures at all and quoting someone else's would only invite you to price ours against it.

08 · The objection

The red flag argument, answered in its own terms.

Start with the part of the objection that is true. No agency controls how a search engine ranks anything, and any page implying a search engine has blessed the practice of guaranteeing rankings is not telling you the truth. If you have read that argument, you read it correctly.

Here is the distinction the whole business rests on. This is not a claim of algorithmic certainty. It is a term about when an invoice is owed.

The published case against this category has three parts, so here are the three answers.

01

"The promise is vague."

Ours names the phrases in the contract before you sign it, and defines page one of Google organic results in an agreed geography as what is being measured.

02

"The remedy is discretionary."

Ours is automatic and monthly. It is triggered by the contract rather than conceded after a complaint, which is the difference between a term and a goodwill gesture.

03

"The agency keeps your money either way."

You are not paying for the attempt in those months.

The terms are the persuasion here. The full argument lives on guarantee, and the specifics of your contract come out on the call.

09 · Questions

Five questions, before the call.

The consultation itself is a conversation. It is not a named package and nothing is reserved.

What does it cost?

It is quoted per business after a competitive review, because what it takes to win a phrase differs by market, by competitor and by the state of your site. There is no figure, range or band published on this site, and you will get yours on the call.

What happens in a month you miss?

The obligation is suspended for that month, not erased, and no money changes hands in either direction. It resumes when results are back on track.

Who chooses the key phrases?

We propose them from competitive research, and they are named in the contract you sign.

Is the web, conversion and design work really included?

Yes, in the one agreed monthly fee. It is included because we are paid on the outcome rather than the hours, which makes an hour of design cost us the same as an hour of anything else.

What is not included?

The boundary of what the fee does not cover has not been stated by the business, and nothing is invented here to fill it. It is one of the things you should ask on the call.
