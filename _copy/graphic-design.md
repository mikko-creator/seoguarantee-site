## S5 why-design-is-included-instead-of-invoiced

<!-- src: D-POSITION-W3, D-INCUMBENT-001, D-MARKET-001 -->

The question a sceptical buyer is actually asking is why this would be free. It is a fair question and it deserves the real answer rather than a warm one.

**1. It is not free.** It sits inside a fee that is owed only while we are delivering the ranking metrics named in your contract. You are paying for it. You are paying for it in the months we deliver, and not in the months we do not.

**2. We are paid on the outcome rather than the hours.** Once that is the model, an hour of design costs us exactly what an hour of anything else costs us, and metering it back to you serves nobody. It would add an invoice, a conversation and a delay to work that only exists to make the campaign land.

**3. The ordinary arrangement is the opposite.** Every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer and prices creative as a separate line or a change-order, which is why a design request usually begins with a quote and a wait. Ours is the one where the retainer stops being owed if the agreed metrics are not delivered, and where the creative work is not a separate purchase at all.

That comparison is structural only. We print no rates, ranges or hourly figures for anyone, ours included, because this site publishes no figures at all.
