## S5 what a Las Vegas campaign covers [L]

One agreed monthly fee covers eight lines of work. Everything Else Is Complementary, and that is a consequence of how we are paid rather than a favour: we are paid on the outcome instead of the hours, so there is no separate web invoice and no change order when the work that moves the number turns out to be a page rebuild instead of a paragraph.

Search Engine Optimization. The phrase set, the pages that have to rank for it, and the technical and editorial work that gets them there, measured in the geography your contract names.

Website Design and Development. Rankings deliver a visitor to a page, and the page decides what happens next. Building it is inside the fee, which is why we never argue about whether a rebuild is in scope.

Content Writing. The pages that answer the searches you are trying to win, written to be read by the person searching rather than assembled for a crawler.

Reputation Management. What a searcher finds when they check you after the click, and the review profile that either supports the enquiry or quietly ends it.

Conversion Optimization. Traffic that does not turn into calls or forms is not a result. This is the work of turning the visits we win into enquiries you can count.

Graphic Design. The visual work the pages and the campaigns need, produced in house rather than quoted separately.

Branding. How the business presents itself across the site and the assets around it, kept consistent so the search result and the page agree with each other.

Consulting. The standing conversation about what the numbers are doing and what to do next, with the person accountable for the terms.

The fee is quoted per business after a competitive review.

## S6 how your key phrases are chosen [M]

Who picks the phrases. We propose the key phrases from competitive research: which businesses hold page one in Las Vegas today, for which searches, and which of those searches produce actual enquiries. They are then named in the contract you sign. We propose, you sign.

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag. Often, and the warnings are aimed at a real problem: a position promised with no terms behind it. What is guaranteed here is a billing condition, tested every month against metrics that are written down before anyone signs. We do not claim to control Google, and we claim no relationship with it.

How long it takes. The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, and the agreement itself is assessed month by month. We will not put a date on a specific phrase, because nobody honestly can.
