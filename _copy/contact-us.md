## S3 what happens on the call

<!-- src: D-POSITION-W2, D-OFFER-018, D-INCUMBENT-001-OBJECTION, D-OFFER-010 -->

**Heading.** What happens on the call.

**1. What we do before we commit to anything.** We look at your market: who currently holds page one for the searches that produce work in your trade, and what those positions are worth to a business your size. Then we propose the key phrases and the metrics they will be judged on, and those are named in the contract you sign. We propose, you sign, and the schedule in the contract is the same document your billing is later judged against.

**2. What we will tell you if the answer is no.** Some markets and some phrases are ones we will not commit to. If yours are among them we will say so on this call, rather than write a contract we cannot deliver against. That is the honest counterweight to a guarantee and it belongs here, on the page where you are deciding to pick up the phone, rather than in a footnote further back.

**3. The terms, one line each.** The retainer is agreed up front and quoted per business after a competitive review. It is assessed month by month against the agreed metrics. Any month we are off target, we keep working and your billing stops, and it resumes when results are back on track.

**On timelines, if it comes up.** The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, and the agreement itself is assessed month by month. [OWNER PLACEHOLDER] Those published windows are the current site's own marketing language, not a ratified contract term, and they are reported as such and nothing more. Nobody here will give you a date by which a specific phrase will rank.

[OWNER PLACEHOLDER] What the fee does not cover has never been stated by this business, on the live site or at intake. It is not invented here. Ask on the call, and it belongs in the agreement in writing.
