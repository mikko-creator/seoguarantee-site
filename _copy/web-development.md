## S3 why-the-build-decides-whether-the-ranking-is-even-possible

<!-- src: D-OFFER-019, D-OFFER-013 -->

Traffic, then leads, then sales. This page is upstream of all three.

**A page that cannot be crawled or indexed cannot rank**, no matter how good the phrase research was. The build sits ahead of everything the contract commits us to, which makes it the first thing worth checking and the last thing worth assuming.

**Speed is not a polish item.** A slow page loses the visit the position bought, and it drags the standing of the pages around it. Treating it as something to look at after launch is how a technically fine site quietly underperforms for a year.

**Which is why the build sits inside the same contract.** If the site is the reason a metric is missed, and the site sits behind a separate quote, then the arrangement is hostage to a purchase you keep deferring. We removed that by including it.

None of this says a build produces a ranking. It says a build decides whether one is possible. The promise stays what it is: page one of Google for the phrases named in your contract, and in a month we are not delivering it, we keep working and your billing stops.
