## S3 capability-deep-dive: the treatment-phrase demand map [M]

The map has four columns: the treatment phrase, the search geography, what the person is deciding at the moment they type it, and who holds page one for it today. The fourth column carries this vertical's least obvious and most useful fact.

That fact is this. A spa competing for a treatment phrase is usually not competing against other spas. The page-one holders for a procedure name are commonly dermatology practices, plastic surgery groups, hospital-affiliated clinics, and national chains running a page per location. The competitor set changes phrase by phrase. That is precisely why a single strategy underperforms here, and it is also the honest answer to the charge that guaranteed search work sells everyone the same plan.

Demand sits in two layers.

**Category phrases.** Med spa with a city, medical spa with a city, medical spa near me. Fewer searches than most owners assume, and the person typing them has already settled the category question.

**Treatment phrases.** Body contouring, laser treatments, injectables, skin resurfacing, hair removal, each with a city or a near me attached. Collectively far larger, fragmented across many separate phrases, and where the decision is actually made. They are also where your service menu genuinely differs from the spa down the road, which makes them the honest place to compete.

Geography: aesthetic clients will travel further than a dental patient and still not across a metro, and demand concentrates around particular neighbourhoods and around the dates people book ahead of. The catchment is something the consultation defines against your own book, not a radius this page invents.

## S6 faq [S]

**Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?**
The published warnings are right about the pattern they describe, and search engines say plainly that no one can guarantee a given ranking. We agree with both. The line we draw is narrow and it is the whole offer: this is a term of business about when an invoice is owed, not a claim about controlling a search engine. No search engine approves of, permits or endorses this arrangement, and nothing here suggests otherwise.

**What tactics do you use, and what will you not do?**
Performed: technical repair of the site, a page for each contracted treatment phrase, the local profile and its categories, internal linking, review flow, and the work on the booking path. Refused: purchased links, private blog networks, spun or padded text, phrase stacking, hidden text, and fabricated reviews. A separate limit worth stating: we do not review or clear your advertising for regulatory compliance, and nothing we do should be treated as that review.

**Who chooses which procedures we go after?**
We propose them from a read of who currently holds page one, and they are named in the contract you sign. You do not hold a veto over the list and cannot swap a phrase in after signature.

**What does a paused month mean?**
The work continues at full scope, the billing stops, and it resumes once results are back on track. The clause is in the contract before signature, so it operates by itself and there is nothing for you to claim.

**How long?**
The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, which are not yet an agreed contract term, and the agreement is assessed month by month throughout. The industry's own published realism band is three to six months and the pattern buyers are warned about is page one in 30 days, so the longer stated window is the honesty signal and is not softened here.
