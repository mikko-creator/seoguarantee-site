## S2 what happens next [M]

1. We call you back within one business day to set a time that works. The call is a conversation, not a presentation.

2. Before that call we look at your market, who currently holds page one for the searches that produce work in your trade, and we come with a proposed set of key phrases and the metrics they will be judged on. We propose them from competitive research, and they are named in the contract you sign.

3. If we agree to work together, the terms are the ones on the guarantee page: a monthly retainer agreed up front, assessed month by month against those named metrics, and any month we are off target we keep working while your billing stops. Nothing here is invented at signing time.

Sometimes the answer on that call is that your phrases are not ones we will commit to, and we will say so rather than sign.
