## S3 why the guarantee exists

<!-- src: D-POSITION-W1, D-OFFER-014 -->

**Heading.** Why the fee is conditional.

Almost every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer that is owed whether or not the agreed result arrives. That is not dishonesty. It is how the category works, and it is a defensible way to sell professional time: you are buying effort, expertise and a plan, and effort was genuinely expended.

But it puts the entire outcome risk on the client. The business owner funds an attempt. If the attempt succeeds, they pay. If it fails, they also pay, and the party best able to judge whether the plan was ever likely to work is the one whose income does not depend on the answer.

This firm is built on the other side of that. The retainer is agreed up front, each month is assessed against the metrics named in the contract, and any month we are off target the work continues while your billing stops. First page on the key phrases named in your contract, or your billing pauses while we keep working. Billing resumes when results are back on track.

What that does to the incentives is the whole point. We carry the commercial risk of our own attempt. It makes us slower to sign, because a contract we cannot deliver costs us the retainer rather than the client. It makes us specific about which phrases go on the schedule, because vague ones are unwinnable and unwinnable ones do not get billed. And it means the plan we propose is the plan we would choose if we were spending our own money on it, because in the months it does not work, we are.

## S5 what we will and will not say

<!-- src: D-INCUMBENT-001-OBJECTION, D-BRAND-RAILS-008 -->

**Heading.** What we will not claim.

You have arrived at the point where you are deciding whether to trust these people. So here is the list of things you will not find us saying, which in this category is more informative than another list of what we do.

- **We do not promise a specific position on a specific date.** First page means page one of Google organic results for an agreed key phrase in an agreed geography. Nothing narrower is promised, because nothing narrower can be.
- **We do not suggest Google is on our side.** Google does not approve, endorse or permit a promise of position, and any agency implying otherwise is telling you something checkable and false.
- **We do not publish case numbers we cannot evidence.** A result gets published when it carries its date range, the method that produced it, the tool it was measured in, and the client's written permission. Until then it is not published in a softer form, it is simply absent.
- **We do not describe the billing pause as money coming back.** Billing pauses and later resumes while the work continues, so the obligation is suspended rather than erased. Calling it anything more generous than that would be overstating our own terms.
- **We do not claim to be the biggest or the best.** The rating below is the real one, count included.
