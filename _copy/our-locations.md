## S2 how we work a market we are not physically in [M]

There is a fair question behind this page. If the desk is in Las Vegas, how does anyone rank a business in Cleveland. Here is the honest answer, in three parts.

The phrase set is local, so the work is local even when the desk is not. We start with competitive research in your market: which businesses hold page one there today, for which searches, and which of those searches actually produce enquiries for a business like yours. From that we propose the key phrases. We propose, you sign, and the phrases are named in the contract. That is the whole of it, and it is worth being plain that there is no veto or substitution right written into the agreement.

The measurement is local too. The contract names the geography a position is measured in, not only the phrase. A ranking is not one number, it moves with where the search happens, and naming the geography is what turns delivered into something checkable.

Then the part most agencies leave out. A local mailing address does not move a ranking. Renting one in your city would add a line to a footer and nothing to your position. We would rather say that than rent an address and let you assume it was doing work. The common complaint about this category is that the promises are vague, and the answer to vagueness is not a nearer postcode, it is terms that are specific and written down before anyone signs.

## S4 what a local campaign actually covers [M]

One agreed monthly fee covers the whole stack: search engine optimization, website design and development, content writing, reputation management, conversion optimization, graphic design, branding and consulting. That breadth is not generosity. It follows from being paid on the outcome rather than on the hours, which is what removes the argument about whether a given piece of work was in scope.

On timing, the current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows. The agreement itself is assessed month by month against the agreed metrics, never as a single test that only settles at the end.

The fee is quoted per business after a competitive review.
