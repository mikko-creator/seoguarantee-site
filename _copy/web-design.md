## S3 why-the-site-is-part-of-the-guarantee

<!-- src: D-POSITION-W1, D-OFFER-019 -->

This is the part that makes the bundling logical rather than generous.

**The page that ranks is a page on your site.** We are contracted to reach page one for named key phrases, and the thing that arrives there is a template, a layout and a piece of writing that you own. The site is not adjacent to the work we are conditioned on. It is the work.

**A site behind a separate quote makes the contract hostage.** If the layout, the structure or the speed of a page is the reason a position cannot be won or held, and fixing it requires you to approve a second purchase first, then the thing our fee depends on is waiting on a decision we cannot make. Including the build removes that failure mode entirely.

**The objectives, in the owner's order.** Get traffic, generate high quality leads, convert those leads to sales. The first is search work. The second and third happen in the design or they do not happen at all.

Nothing here says the design itself is guaranteed. What is promised is the position, and the fee for it stops in any month it is not delivered while the work carries on.

## S4 what-we-build

<!-- src: D-BRAND-RAILS-016, D-BRAND-RAILS-018 -->

This page owns the site as a designed artefact. Here is what that actually means.

**A full redesign or rebuild of the public site, not a theme swap.** That means the page templates a campaign genuinely needs rather than the ones a theme happened to include: service pages, location pages, vertical pages and a contact page that behaves like a contact page.

**The brand applied consistently across every template.** Palette, type, spacing and imagery treatment carried through the whole site, so a visitor who lands on a service page from a search meets the same business a visitor who lands on the home page meets.

**Mobile and tablet layouts designed rather than inherited.** The majority of the traffic a campaign wins arrives on a phone, and a layout that was drawn at desktop width and then allowed to reflow is a different experience from one that was drawn for the phone in the first place.

**Ongoing monthly design changes as the campaign moves.** Every month you need updates to your site, and we get it. That is the basis of this entire page: the design work is a standing capacity rather than a project with a delivery date, and it keeps running in a month billing is paused.

**Client portal access** to request that work and track where each request has got to.

Two neighbouring inventories deliberately do not live here. The integrations and business automations behind the site belong to web-development. The navigation and toolbar behaviour, and what a visitor can actually do once a page loads, belong to ui-ux-design. Each is one sentence and a link on this page, and nothing more, so the two pages do not compete for the same enquiry.

## S5 builds-that-do-not-cost-you-rankings

<!-- src: D-MARKET-002, D-INCUMBENT-001 -->

A redesign is the most common way a business loses positions it already had. If that has happened to you, you already know it, and it is a reasonable thing to be afraid of.

Here is what we hold to on a rebuild. URLs and redirects are planned before launch rather than reconstructed afterwards from what broke. Content and internal links are carried over deliberately, page by page, rather than left behind in the old template. Page speed is treated as a design constraint while the design is being made, not as a cleanup task after it ships. And nothing goes live that the site cannot then be measured on.

There is a fair test of all of this, and buyer-education content in this category actively tells you to run it: put an agency's own website through a speed test before you believe anything it says about yours. That is a reasonable thing to do and we would rather you did it. This page has to pass the standards it just claimed.

We publish no speed, uptime or vitals figure here. A number without the date it was measured, the tool it was measured in and the page it was measured on is not usable copy, and none of that is on file.
