## S3 capability-deep-dive: the search-to-estimate path [M]

Two halves, contracted together, because in this trade they only work as a pair.

**Half one, the phrase set.** Demand splits four ways.

Trade-and-metro phrases: the trade name plus a city or a suburb. The core commercial set, heavily contested, and where the metro's established firms already sit.

Job-scoped phrases: a specific scope of work plus a location. Lower volume per phrase, far higher intent, and the tier where your actual specialism separates you from a general listing.

Cost and research phrases: what a job costs, how long it takes, what is involved. Very large volume, poor direct conversion, and valuable because they reach a buyer weeks before they are ready to request anything. These are a different job from the commercial set and should be contracted as such rather than blended into one promise.

Homeowner against commercial and general-contractor demand: two audiences sharing a trade name and sharing almost no phrases. A firm serving both is running two sets.

**Half two, the request for an estimate**, which is inside the same agreed fee and is where this page differs from every other one on this site. The conversion work here is concrete and checkable: the request form and how much it demands before it will let somebody submit, whether photographs can be attached, whether a scope can be described in the buyer's own words, how fast a callback is promised and whether that promise is kept, and the project gallery a homeowner uses to work out whether you build things that look like the thing they want. That is a scope of work. No conversion figure is attached to it, because no such figure exists anywhere we could cite.

The two halves map onto the objectives in the order they were given to us: get traffic, generate high quality leads, convert those leads to sales. This is the one page in the vertical set where the middle term carries the weight, because in contracting the gap between a visit and a lead is where the money is lost.

Method of proposal: we read the firms currently holding page one for each candidate phrase, propose a list from that read, and then the list is signed. Signing is what makes "delivered" checkable instead of arguable.

## S5 faq [S]

**Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?**
The published warnings describe a real pattern and they are worth reading. No one can guarantee where a page lands and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What is guaranteed here is a term of business about when an invoice is owed, not control over a search engine. No search engine approves of, permits or endorses this arrangement.

**What will you do, and what will you not do?**
Done: technical repair of the site, a page for each contracted phrase, the local profile and its categories, internal linking, review flow, and the rebuild of the request form itself. Not done: purchased links, private blog networks, spun or padded text, phrase stacking, hidden text, and fabricated reviews. Any proposal containing one of those has told you what it is.

**Who chooses the phrases?**
We propose them from a read of the current page one and they are named in the contract you sign. No veto, and nothing gets swapped in after signature.

**My sales cycle is two months. What does a paused month mean when the revenue has not landed yet?**
Answered without sleight of hand. The monthly assessment runs against the agreed phrases and their positions, not against your closed jobs. We do not control your bid pricing, your crew capacity or your close rate, and we will not pretend to. That limit is also your protection: the position is checkable this month regardless of where the revenue has got to. Off-target, the work continues and the billing stops.

**How long?**
The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, which are not yet an agreed contract term, and the agreement is assessed month by month throughout. The industry's published realism band is three to six months and the pattern buyers are warned about is page one in 30 days, so a longer stated window is the honesty signal rather than a weakness.
