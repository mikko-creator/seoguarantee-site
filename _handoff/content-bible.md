## §3b — DON'T-CLAIM list (hard prohibitions)

- Never claim or imply Google approves, endorses, or permits ranking guarantees.
- Never state the ranking promise without the payment condition in the same breath.
- Never describe the pause as a refund, a money-back guarantee, or "risk-free" — billing pauses and later resumes; the obligation is suspended, not erased.
- Never publish a client veto or substitution right over key phrases (owner ruled: agency proposes, client signs).
- Never state a percentage, multiple, or count without its time period and source.
- Never name a client without written permission on file.
- Never display a press or partner logo without a live link to the coverage and confirmed rights.
- Never state an absolute success rate in any vertical. **This binds the safe versions in §2 as well as the live-site strings they replace** — a rewrite that says "every campaign we have run" is the same prohibited claim in calmer words (P7 blocker 7). A bounded "[k] of [n], between [dates]" is permitted; a rate, a percentage, or a universal is not.
- Never assert the 6- or 12-month guarantee window as a **term of the contract** — it is unratified (§3, P7 blocker 9). Reporting what the current site says is permitted; asserting it as agreed terms is not.
- Never state or imply that the billing pause **has already been operated** on a live account until the owner confirms the account, the months and the client's consent (P7 blocker 13).
- Never make an earnings claim about what a prospect will make.
- Never publish a price, including "starting at" (§F1).
- Never imply physical offices in the location metros.
- **Never state a count of metros holding a paying client** (P7 blocker 12) — not "25 US metros", not "the 25 metros we serve", not "26", not a spelled-out ("twenty-five", "twenty-six"), rounded, hedged or "over/nearly [n]" variant, and in no surface: body copy, headings, page titles, metas, nav labels, card counts or structured data. **25 is a count of PUBLISHED CITY PAGES** on the current site (`/our-locations/`, counted 2026-08-11) **and nothing more**; how many of those metros hold a paying client is evidenced nowhere in the corpus and is `[OWNER-DATA]`. This is a **separate prohibition from the offices rule above** — that rule bans claiming a physical presence, this one bans claiming a serviced-market count, and a sentence can clear the first while breaching the second. The only permitted coverage sentence is the numberless §2 form. A number returns only on owner-supplied data carrying its period and source (§1 "numbers always carry their frame"), never inferred from the page count. **Rendering the 25 metro CARDS on `our-locations` is not a breach** — the card set is the published-page list and is labelled as such; asserting the count as clients is.
- Never use an exclamation mark or emoji (owner: remove all).
- Never reproduce the live typos ("desireable") or broken headline grammar ("WHY OUR MARKETING IS AMAZING?").
