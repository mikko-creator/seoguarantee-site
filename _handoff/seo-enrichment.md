## §3 — Structured-data ordinance (new in P5)

The live site carries an unqualified guarantee claim in **JSON-LD across 46 of 49 captured pages**:

> `"Our Las Vegas SEO Service is guaranteed to bring you to the first page of Google!"`

This is the highest-reach claim on the entire site and it appears in machine-readable metadata, where it is trivially harvested. It must not carry forward. Replacement description, conditioned and without the exclamation:

> `First-page SEO on the key phrases agreed in your contract. Any month we are off target we keep working and your billing pauses until we are back on target.`

**Why this string changed (readiness-verdict blocker 6 — REPAIRED 2026-08-12).** The previous replacement read `SEO with a first-page guarantee on agreed key phrases — or you don't pay.` That was written in this file and it was wrong: it asserts a **terminal** non-payment remedy, and D-003 contains no such remedy. The agreement is a monthly retainer assessed month by month; off target, the billing **pauses and later resumes** while the work continues. A refusal to pay that ends the obligation is a different commercial promise from a suspension of it, and this string is the single highest-reach place on the site to make that error — it currently sits in JSON-LD on **46 of 49 captured pages**, machine-readable and trivially scraped into an AI answer or a complaint. The replacement carries all three load-bearing facts: the work continues, the billing pauses, the billing resumes. 155 characters, no exclamation, no superlative.

**This string is normative.** Where the build emits a `description` in `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, or `WebSite` JSON-LD, it uses the text above verbatim or a shortening that still contains the pause. A shortening that drops the pause is the defect this row exists to prevent.

Also removed site-wide: **"Best In Class Web Design Guarantee"** (31 pages — an unqualified superlative and a *second*, competing guarantee) and the plural CTA **"WANT OUR SEO GUARANTEES?"** (29 pages — the guarantee is singular and definite, `content-bible` §5).
