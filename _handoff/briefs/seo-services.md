## §6 how-each-month-is-assessed — M
The mechanic, in four beats, stated as a term of business and not as a claim about controlling an algorithm.

1. A monthly retainer is agreed up front.
2. Each month is assessed against the metrics named in the contract.
3. In a month we are off-target, the work continues and your billing stops.
4. Billing resumes when results are back on track.

Say plainly that this is a term about when an invoice is owed, not a prediction. Permitted phrasings: "your billing stops", "we keep working, you stop paying", "nothing is refunded because nothing is billed". Banned outright: refund, money-back, risk-free, "there is no invoice", "nothing to refund". The pause suspends the obligation; it does not erase one.

One honest comparison is allowed here, structural only: every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer, and the ordinary version of that retainer is owed whether or not the agreed positions arrive. Ours is the one that stops being owed. Do not print any competitor's rates, ranges or hourly figures on either side of that comparison — the site publishes no figures at all, and printing someone else's invites the reader to price ours against it.
