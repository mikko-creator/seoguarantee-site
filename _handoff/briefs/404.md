## Outcome
FEATURE -> DO -> MEANS: FEATURE: a 404 that returns a real 404 status and routes to the four destinations the retired URLs actually came from — the guarantee, the metros page, results and the vertical hub — plus a full index of the public site -> DO: land on the thing you were looking for even when a redirect is missing -> MEANS: 138 retired URLs cannot silently dead-end on the website of a company that sells search visibility.

## §1 hero — the plain not-found message — S
Three lines, in the same plainspoken register as every other page. This is the page where brands most often break their own voice, and this one will not.

- A heading that says the page is not here. "Page not found" or "That page has moved or retired."
- One sentence of explanation with no apology theatre: the site was rebuilt and a number of old pages were consolidated, so this address may have been one of them.
- One sentence of instruction: start from one of the routes below, or call and we will point you to it.

Banned outright on this page: "Oops", "Uh oh", "Looks like you're lost", a broken-robot illustration, a 404-themed joke, a cartoon, a large decorative numeral treated as the hero art, an animation. No exclamation mark, no emoji.

Required data: D-BRAND-RAILS-004, D-BRAND-RAILS-006, D-OFFER-001

## §2 the four places retired URLs came from — M
Four routes, chosen to match where the retired traffic actually originates rather than to mirror the main navigation. Each gets a label and one line of explanation.

1. The guarantee — for anything offer-shaped. Label it as terms, never as a bare promise: "The guarantee: what we agree, how each month is assessed, and when you do not pay." A 404 must never carry an unconditioned ranking claim, and this is the single most likely place for one to slip in.
2. The metros page and the Las Vegas page — for the 25 retired city URLs, which are the largest single block of retired addresses. One line: the city pages were consolidated into one coverage page listing the metros we cover, and Las Vegas, our home market, has its own page. State no metro count in that line — ⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER, 25 counts the retired city pages, not markets with a paying client (readiness-verdict blocker 12).
3. Results — for the 20 retired portfolio item URLs, the 5 taxonomy archives and the 4 magazine-forward pages. One line: client outcomes now live in one place, each with its date range and method.
4. Industries — for the retired vertical paths. One line: the trade pages moved to shorter addresses.

Each route is a link with a real label. No route may be a bare "click here" and none may be styled as a primary CTA competing with the others.

Required data: D-IA-TIER1, D-IA-TIER2, D-POSITION-W1

## §3 the full site index — S
A static, complete list of every public page, grouped and plainly labelled. This is what makes the page genuinely useful rather than decorative, and it is what a visitor uses when none of the four routes is the thing they wanted.

Group as: the offer (index, guarantee, full-suite and the seven service pages) · proof and trades (results, industries and the five vertical pages) · where we work (our-locations, las-vegas-seo) · the firm (about, videos, seo-blog) · contact (contact-us).

If there is no search backend, ship this static index and do NOT ship a search field. A search box that returns nothing on a 404 page, on the site of a company selling search, is worse than no search box.

Required data: D-IA-TIER3, D-BRAND-RAILS-016

One quiet line at the foot with the phone number (702) 420-7272: if none of the above is what you were looking for, call and we will point you to it. Deliberately understated — subordinate to the routing above, not a conversion band.

No form on this page. No lead magnet. No offer restated.

## Claims and constraints

Technical contract — this is as binding as the copy.
- The page must return HTTP 404. The 14 retired internal ops pages and the 3 test pages return 410, because they have no honest destination. Never a soft 404 with HTTP 200 — a site selling SEO that soft-404s fails its own pitch the first time anyone inspects it.
- noindex, and excluded from the sitemap.

Scope discipline. This page is a safety net, not a substitute for the redirect map. The full DROP+301 and MERGE ordinance must still be implemented. If a retired URL reaches this page in production, a redirect is missing — that is a defect to fix, not a user experience to design around.

One related live defect this page must not be used to paper over: the current homepage links to /about-us/ twice, and /about-us/ is itself a live 404. Both links are repaired in the build AND the old URL is redirected to about. Routing that traffic here instead would be hiding a broken link behind a recovery page.

HOLDS rows that bind this page.
- The routing link to the guarantee is labelled as terms. The ranking promise never appears here without the payment condition, and the cleanest solution is that it does not appear here at all.
- No proof claims of any kind — no percentages, no client names, no rating, no press. A 404 is not a sales page.
- "The best SEO company" and every superlative are banned, as everywhere.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- No price.

Owner-pending on this page: nothing blocking. This page can be built and shipped as specified, which makes it one of the few pages in the set with no open owner dependency.
