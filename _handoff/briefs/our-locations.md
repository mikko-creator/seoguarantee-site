## §2 how we work a market we are not physically in — M
The section that earns the consolidation. It answers the objection a buyer forms the moment they notice we have one office and a national metro list: how can a Las Vegas agency rank me in Cleveland.

Answer it in three concrete moves, in this order.
1. The phrase set is local, so the work is local even when the desk is not. We propose the key phrases from competitive research in your metro — who currently holds page one there, for which searches, and what those searches are worth to a business like yours. They are then named in the contract you sign. We propose, you sign; there is no client veto or substitution right, and the copy must not imply one.
2. The measurement is local. The contract names the geography the position is measured in, not just the phrase, which is what makes "delivered" checkable rather than arguable.
3. What being in your city would not add. Say plainly that a local mailing address does not move a ranking, and that we would rather be honest about that than rent one. This is the page's direct answer to the published attack that guarantee sellers are vague — the whole point is that the terms are specific and written down before anyone signs.

Register: plainspoken and specific, not loud. No caps-locked headline, no rhetorical-question headline, no exclamation.
