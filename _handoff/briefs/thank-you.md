## §2 what happens next — three steps — M
Three numbered steps and nothing more, each one sentence long, written as commitments the call must actually honour.

1. We call you back within one business day to set a time that works. The call is a conversation, not a presentation.
2. Before that call we look at your market — who currently holds page one for the searches that produce work in your trade — and we come with a proposed set of key phrases and the metrics they will be judged on. We propose them from competitive research, and they are named in the contract you sign. Write it in exactly that shape. Never "you choose the phrases", never "you approve them", never "strike any you don't believe in" — this page is where a buyer forms their expectation of the call, and publishing a veto here would be contradicted by the contract at signing.
3. If we agree to work together, the terms are the ones on the guarantee page: a monthly retainer agreed up front, assessed month by month against those named metrics, and any month we are off-target we keep working while your billing stops. Nothing here is invented at signing time.

Add one honest sentence at the end of the three: sometimes the answer on that call is that your phrases are not ones we will commit to, and we will say so rather than sign.

Required data: D-POSITION-W2, D-OFFER-010, D-INCUMBENT-001-OBJECTION

## §3 while you wait — three things worth reading — S
Reading, deliberately — not conversion. Three links, each with one line explaining why it is worth the two minutes before the call.

- The guarantee terms, so the contract language is familiar before anyone talks about it. Label the link as terms, not as a promise: what we agree, how each month is assessed, and when you do not pay.
- The results page, so you can see what a named, dated outcome looks like in a trade near yours, with the method that produced it stated.
- The videos, so you can see the person who signs the contract explain the mechanic in his own words before you hear it on a call.

No fourth link, no downloads, no newsletter, no calendar embed, no upsell. This section is the whole reason the page is worth building instead of shipping a bare thanks.

Required data: D-POSITION-W1, D-IA-TIER1, D-MARKET-001-PROOF
