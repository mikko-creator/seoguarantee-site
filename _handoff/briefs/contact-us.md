## §3 what happens on the call — M
The section that converts scepticism, and the only place on the site where the limits of the offer are stated plainly. A buyer who has read the guarantee page arrives here still asking one question: what will they actually commit to, and what will they refuse.

Three beats.
1. What we do before we commit. We look at your market, at who currently holds page one for the searches that produce work in your trade, and at what those positions are worth. We then propose the key phrases and the metrics they will be judged on, and they are named in the contract you sign. We propose, you sign — do not write "you choose", "you approve" or "you can strike any you don't believe in".
2. What we will tell you if the answer is no. Say plainly that some markets and some phrases are ones we will not commit to, and that we would rather say so on this call than sign a contract we cannot deliver against. This is the honest counterweight to a guarantee and it belongs on the conversion page, not buried.
3. What the terms are, in one line each: the retainer is agreed up front, it is assessed month by month, and any month we are off-target we keep working while your billing stops. If the timeline comes up, say that the current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows and that the agreement itself is assessed month by month — never a date by which a specific phrase will rank. ⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER — the real contract window is UNRATIFIED (content-bible §3b); the windows may be reported only as what the current site says, never as a contract term.
