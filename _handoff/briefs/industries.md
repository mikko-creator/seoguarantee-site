## §2 why-this-trade-is-different-wedge — M

The defensive core of the page, and the reason it cannot be thin.

The category's most visible published attack on guarantee sellers is that "one-size-fits-all SEO strategies don't work" — and the page carrying that attack ranks for this brand's own category term. Per-vertical substance here is therefore a requirement, not a nicety. A generic hub on this site is not merely weak, it confirms the specific accusation a prospect has probably already read.

State plainly what actually differs by trade, in four concrete dimensions, and use them as the page's organising spine:
1. The phrase set — what people type is completely different, and so is what they mean by it.
2. The buying moment behind the search — an emergency at 11pm is not a considered purchase researched over three weeks.
3. The geography that matters — a radius around one chair, versus a metro, versus a multi-state practice.
4. What a lead is worth — which is what decides how hard a phrase is worth fighting for, and therefore which phrases we propose.

This is also the page's own confession point, and it should be written as one rather than hidden. The current per-vertical pages are near-identical to each other, and the rebuild is what fixes that. The honest version of this section earns the reader's attention by being specific in the very place the old site was generic. Do not make a claim about the old site on the page — just be conspicuously specific.

Answer the objection directly and without defensiveness: a guarantee written against phrases chosen for the wrong trade would be worthless to us, because we only get paid while the agreed metrics are delivered. The incentive is the argument.

## §3 sub-area-blocks — the five trades — S

Exactly FIVE blocks, one per routed leaf, and every block links to its deep page. This is the T-HUB floor.

Each block carries ONE genuinely trade-specific line naming a real phrase family for that trade, plus the buying moment behind it. The shared frame across all five, stated once at the top of the section and not repeated five times: we propose the key phrases from competitive research and they are named in the contract you sign.

- Dental -> industries-dental. Verified phrase families from live client work: "emergency dentistry", "dentist near me", "oral surgery [city]". Buying moment splits hard between emergency and elective, and the phrase set splits with it.
- Law firm -> industries-law-firm. Verified live phrase families: personal injury, car accident, business law, with practice-area and multi-city footprints (the live copy references personal injury attorneys operating across Las Vegas and Phoenix). One case can justify a year of the fee, which is why the phrase competition is brutal.
- Medical spa -> industries-medical-spa. Verified live treatment phrases: "body contouring", "laser treatments". Treatment-led demand, and the search is for the procedure before it is for the provider.
- HVAC -> industries-hvac. The repair call when the system quits, plus seasonal replacement demand. FLAG: no captured source language exists for this trade.
- Contracting -> industries-contracting. Homeowners and builders searching for bids and estimates. FLAG: no captured source language exists for this trade either.

## §4 proof-data-point — S

The single proof element, and the discipline it needs is exactness about what is actually proven.

The proof that ships is the CONTRACT MECHANIC itself, stated as contract language: the retainer is agreed up front, each month is assessed against the agreed metrics, an off-target month pauses the billing while the work continues, and billing resumes when results are back on track. That is owner-ruled and fully publishable today, and it needs no precedent to stand up.

⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER — THE PAUSE PRECEDENT IS UNVERIFIED AND MAY NOT BE ASSERTED HERE. What IS verified is that the client's own live law-firm page CONTAINS this sentence: when things initially went wrong with a client's campaign due to external factors, "we paused their payments until we had their results back on track." What is NOT verified is that the episode happened — the sentence is that page's own rank-3 marketing copy, on the very case card this spec rules unusable, and content-bible §3b bans stating or implying the pause has already been operated on a live account until the owner confirms the account, the months and the client's consent.

Until that confirmation lands, do not write it as an account of something that happened, do not date it, do not name a client, and do not call it the difference between a brochure promise and a real invoice. If it is referenced at all it is reported as what the current site SAYS, visibly flagged as unconfirmed, with the routing to the law-firm leaf that holds the same flag.

If a timeline is paired with it, say that the current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows and that the agreement itself is assessed month by month. What is verified is that the live site publishes those windows — not that they are agreed terms. ⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER — the real contract window is UNRATIFIED (content-bible §3b); report the windows only as what the current site says, never as a term of the agreement.

State the pause correctly every time it appears: billing PAUSES while off-target and RESUMES when results are back on track, and the work continues throughout. Never a refund, never money-back, never risk-free, never "there is no invoice". Overstating it hands the category's published attack exactly the target it is aiming at.

No percentages in this section.
