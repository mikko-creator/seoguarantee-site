## §3 why-brand-work-belongs-inside-a-ranking-contract — M
The load-bearing argument of the page: the honest answer to "why would branding be included". Four beats.

1. It is not a gift. It is inside a fee that is owed only while the contracted ranking metrics are being delivered.
2. We are paid on the outcome rather than the hours. Once that is the model, we have a direct interest in the thing the traffic lands on being credible, because a position that produces nothing is a position we still have to deliver next month.
3. The buyer we are built for is an owner whose competitors are consistently ranking for the phrases that matter. Taking that position puts you side by side with those competitors in the same result list, being compared on the strength of what you look like. The brand is what gets compared.
4. So the sequence runs traffic, then high-quality leads, then sales — and the brand is what decides whether beat two survives beat one.

Wherever the ranking promise is touched, the payment condition travels in the same sentence. Do not claim a brand-driven uplift of any kind.

The mechanic, in four beats, stated as a term of business rather than a claim about controlling an algorithm.

3. In a month we are off-target, the brand work continues and your billing stops.
